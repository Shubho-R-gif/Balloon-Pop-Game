# 🎈 Balloon Pop Game  

A **fun and interactive** balloon popping game built with **React**, **Vite**, and **Konva**. Pop balloons, avoid the **Boom**, and challenge yourself to beat your high score!

  <img src="assets/screenshot.png" alt="Balloon Pop Game Screenshot" width="600"/>
</p>

---

## 🚀 Features  

✅ **Smooth Animations** – Powered by **Konva**  
✅ **Play & Pause Buttons** – Start or pause the game anytime  
✅ **Interactive Gameplay** – Click balloons to **score points**  
✅ **Boom Mechanic** – Click on a **Boom** and the game is over  
✅ **Scoring System** – Missed balloons **decrease** score  
✅ **High Score Storage** – Best score saved in **Local Storage**  
✅ **Music Control** – Toggle background music ON/OFF  

---

## 🎮 How to Play  

🎯 **Click** on balloons to **pop** them and earn points.  
💥 **Avoid** clicking on the **Boom**, or the game **ends**.  
🎵 **Control Music** from the **Settings Menu**.  
⏸️ **Pause** the game anytime and return to the **Main Menu**.  
🏆 **High Score** is saved so you can keep beating your best!
